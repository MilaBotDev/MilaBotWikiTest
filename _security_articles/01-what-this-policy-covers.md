## 1. What this policy covers

This policy covers the current official MilaBot service operated by Mila, including its commands, interactive controls, and supporting systems. Older versions and independently operated copies are not maintained under this policy.

Examples of security issues include:

- Exposed bot tokens, API keys, or hosting credentials.
- Unauthorized access to messages, tickets, personal information, or stored records.
- Bypasses of moderation permissions, role restrictions, participant checks, or active privacy restrictions.
- Forged interactions or controls that perform actions as another user.
- Exploits that duplicate virtual currency or items, manipulate another user's progress, or bypass purchase and payout safeguards.
- Vulnerabilities that could disrupt the Bot or its supporting services.

Ordinary support questions, privacy requests, and server moderation appeals should follow the contact instructions in our [Terms of Service]({{ '/tos/' | relative_url }}) and [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
