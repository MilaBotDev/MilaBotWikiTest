## 3. Keep investigation safe

Use accounts, servers, and data you control or have explicit permission to test. Stop if you encounter another person's private information, and report the exposure without copying or sharing more of it.

Do not disrupt the service, send spam, attempt mass data extraction, change other users' records, or spend or transfer rewards obtained through an exploit. This policy does not grant permission to access systems or accounts without authorization, or to bypass Discord's rules.

Please give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address the issue before publishing technical details that would enable abuse. Coordinate disclosure with staff through the private reporting route.
