## 2. How to report an issue

Visit the **[MilaBot Discord support server](https://discord.gg/Gff3rasUn3)** and tell staff you need to submit a private security report. Ask for a private reporting route before sharing details; do not post vulnerabilities or personal information in public channels.

In your private report, include what you know:

- The affected command or feature and a brief explanation of the risk.
- When you noticed it and the steps that led to it.
- What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
- Relevant message links or IDs, and redacted screenshots or logs if helpful.
- Whether you believe credentials or another person's information were exposed.

You do not need to exploit an issue further to report it. If you encounter an exposed credential, explain where it was found without posting the credential itself or trying to use it.
