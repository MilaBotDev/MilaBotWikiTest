## 4. What happens next

We will review reports, assess their impact, and prioritize action according to the risk. We may request clarification, fix code or configuration, rotate credentials, restrict affected features, or temporarily take the service offline.

We will provide updates through the agreed contact route where practical. We do not promise a fixed response or resolution time, and this policy does not offer a paid bug bounty. Any applicable legal or platform notification deadlines still apply.

Where an incident requires notifying affected users, Discord, or relevant authorities, we will do so as required. Information submitted in a report is handled as described in our [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
