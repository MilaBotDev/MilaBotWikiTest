## Two separate level systems

**Patisserie level** determines how many treats each bake gives. Spend Expansion Orbs from customers to upgrade it, up to level 100.

**Vitrine level** belongs to each equipped slot and improves combat stats, up to level 100. Each additional level adds 5 HP and 3 attack before percentage plate bonuses. Replacing or clearing the treat resets that slot.
