## Board and strategy games

| Game | How to start | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Knucklebones]({{ '/wiki/knucklebones/' | relative_url }}) | /knucklebones | Place dice in columns, combine matches, and destroy enemy dice. |
| [Expanded Knucklebones]({{ '/wiki/knucklebonesexpanded/' | relative_url }}) | /knucklebonesexpanded | Add special dice, optional items, and a 4×4 board option. |
| [Flockade]({{ '/wiki/flockade/' | relative_url }}) | /flockade | Draft pieces and use blessings to win duels. |
| [Battlesheep]({{ '/wiki/battlesheep/' | relative_url }}) | /battlesheep | Hide a flock on a private board and attack the opposing sea. |
| [4 In A Row]({{ '/wiki/4inarow/' | relative_url }}) | /4inarow | Connect four pieces before your opponent. |
| [AuctionWar]({{ '/wiki/auctionwar/' | relative_url }}) | /auctionwar | Bid on relics with different scoring rules. |
| [ExpansionWar]({{ '/wiki/expansionwar/' | relative_url }}) | /expansionwar | Move across the map to claim territory. |

Two-player games in this table support an optional human opponent; leaving that option empty starts a bot match. AuctionWar and ExpansionWar can fill empty seats with bots.

## Cards and chance

**/blackmila** plays blackjack with Hit and Stand controls. **/sloths** plays slots. **/coinflip**, **/d6**, and **/d20** provide quick random results.

For games that wager your saved Milacoins, see [virtual wagering]({{ '/wiki/wagering/' | relative_url }}).

## Game controls

Use the Help control inside a match for its rules. Where offered, Replay starts another game and Give Up ends your participation. Turn controls belong to the participating players; other users cannot take your move for you.
