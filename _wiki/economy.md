## Daily and weekly claims

**/daily** has a base reward of **100 Milacoins**. **/weekly** has a base reward of **500 Milacoins**. Your Patisserie collection adds percentage bonuses for each distinct treat owned.

The bot uses a **fixed UTC+01:00** clock. Daily claims reset at midnight on that clock; weekly claims reset on Monday at midnight. This is a calendar reset, not a rolling 24-hour or seven-day timer, and the fixed offset does not change for daylight saving time.

## Balance and rankings

Use **/money** to check your balance. **/leaderboard** can show currency or Expansion Orbs, with worldwide or current-server scope. The currency ranking shows the top ten and can also report your position.

Use **/privacyanddata** for worldwide leaderboard opt-out controls. Read the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for the full explanation.

## Spending and earning

Buy plates with Milacoins in **/shop**. ShowOff battles also award coins. Expansion Orbs are a separate progression resource earned from customer orders and spent on bakery upgrades.
