These are level 1 stats without a plate. The collection bonus applies once per distinct owned treat; duplicates do not stack.

## How to obtain

Use **/bake**. Pride Month Fest: 06-01 inclusive to 06-30 exclusive (UTC). Baking first rolls an active rarity, then chooses equally among its available treats. See [rarities and drop rates]({{ '/wiki/rarities/' | relative_url }}).

## Use in your Vitrine

Choose this treat through the **/vitrine** editor, or use **Set treat** with ID <code>cake_bi</code> and a slot from 1 to 3. You must own it, and cannot put the same treat in two slots. Replacing a treat resets that slot’s level, XP, and plate.
