## How to use

Type **/conversion** in Discord and select MilaBot’s command. Required inputs are marked below; optional inputs can be left blank.

## Options


<table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Option</th><th scope="col">Type</th><th scope="col">Required</th><th scope="col">What it does</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr data-wiki-entry><td><code>value</code></td><td>Number</td><td>Yes</td><td>The number to convert.</td></tr>
<tr data-wiki-entry><td><code>from</code></td><td>Text</td><td>Yes</td><td>The unit to convert from.<br>Suggestions appear while typing.</td></tr>
<tr data-wiki-entry><td><code>to</code></td><td>Text</td><td>Yes</td><td>The unit to convert to.<br>Suggestions appear while typing.</td></tr></tbody></table>
