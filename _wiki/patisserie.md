## Bake and collect

**/bake** rolls one treat per Patisserie level, every **three hours**. A new bakery starts at level 1. Results may include duplicates; your collection does not influence drop odds.

**/patisserie** shows the treats you have discovered and their bonuses. Each distinct owned treat adds a rarity-dependent percentage to daily and weekly claims. Copies of the same treat do not stack that bonus.

- [Treat catalogue]({{ '/wiki/treats/' | relative_url }}): individual stats, IDs, and artwork.
- [Rarities and odds]({{ '/wiki/rarities/' | relative_url }}): weighted drop chances and seasonal treats.

## Grow the bakery

The daily **/customer** asks for a specific kind of treat. A correct answer earns five Expansion Orbs; a wrong one loses five, down to zero. Both use the day’s attempt. You need to own the selected treat, but serving it does not consume it.

Use the Patisserie upgrade control to spend Orbs. Higher bakery levels give more treats per bake, up to 100 per bake at level 100. The cooldown remains three hours.

- [Customer answers]({{ '/wiki/customers/' | relative_url }}) are hidden behind expandable clues.
- [Level requirements]({{ '/wiki/progression/' | relative_url }}) list every upgrade cost.

## Build a Vitrine

Your **/vitrine** holds three different treats. Add plates to improve their stats and unlock passive abilities. **/showoff** uses this team in automatic battles and grants XP to occupied slots.

Bakery level and Vitrine levels are separate: one determines baking quantity, while the others determine combat strength.
