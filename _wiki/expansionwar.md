## Set up

Use **/expansionwar** and choose **2, 3, or 4 players** and a **Peace or War** variant. Add human opponents where desired; bots fill the remaining seats.

## Movement and territory

Move your circular player marker with the arrow buttons. The tile you leave becomes yours. Walls and other players block movement.

- **Peace:** enemy territory also blocks movement. The game can end when the board is claimed or movement is blocked.
- **War:** you can enter and capture enemy territory. The configured limit is **25 rounds**.

The player or players with the most points at the end win. The supplied configuration contains three map layouts.
