## Play

Run **/4inarow**, optionally selecting another user. With no opponent selected, MilaBot plays the other side.

Choose a column on your turn. Pieces fall into the next available position; a full column cannot accept another piece. Connect four horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.

## Plan your moves

Check for an immediate winning move, then for a line your opponent can complete next turn. A move can create more than one threat, so inspect diagonals as well as rows and columns.

Use the game’s Help and Replay controls for rules and another round.
