## Take a turn

Run **/knucklebones**, optionally choosing an opponent. Each player has a 3×3 board. On your turn, place the rolled six-sided die in a column with space remaining.

Placing a die destroys matching values in the opponent’s corresponding column. Choose between building your own score and removing theirs.

## Scoring

Within a column, each die’s value is multiplied by the number of dice showing that same value. A column containing **4, 1, 4** scores **4×2 + 1 + 4×2 = 17**. Three sixes in one column score **54**.

The game ends when either board is full. The higher total score wins.
