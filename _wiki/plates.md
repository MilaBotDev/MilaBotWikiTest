## Equipping plates

Buy a plate from **/shop**, then assign it to an occupied Vitrine slot. Each equipped copy is reserved for one slot; equip the same plate in multiple slots only if you own enough copies.

## Passive abilities

### Heart Chocolate Burst

<img class="wiki-emoji" src="{{ '/assets/wiki/VoidHeart.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Heart Chocolate Burst" width="48" height="48" loading="lazy"> *Love fills the area:* heals 8% of maximum HP before this treat attacks.

Granted by: Sakura Blossom Plate.

### Vampire Fang

 Heals for 13% of damage dealt. *Bleh, bleh-bleh!*

Granted by: Honeycomb Plate, Silver Plate.

### Sugar Shield

 Takes 26% less damage.

Granted by: Crystal Plate, Snowflake Plate.

### $!$? Strike

 Each hit has a 13% chance to deal **double damage**, *because f- you!*

Defined in the bot, but no current plate grants this ability.

### Moon's Blessing

<img class="wiki-emoji" src="{{ '/assets/wiki/MoonFragment.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Moon's Blessing" width="48" height="48" loading="lazy"> /ᐠ > ˕ <マ⊃━☆ﾟ.*･｡ﾟRevives the first treat to die on your team.

Revives at 50% maximum HP. Granted by: Rainbow Plate, Strawberry Plate.

### Double or Nothing

<img class="wiki-emoji" src="{{ '/assets/wiki/MilaCoinSpin.gif' | relative_url }}" alt="Double or Nothing" width="48" height="48" loading="lazy"> Each turn, this treat has a 50/50 chance to attack twice or do nothing. *Suits the true gamblers:3*

Granted by: Chocolate Plate.
