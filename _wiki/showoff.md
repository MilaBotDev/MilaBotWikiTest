## Starting a battle

Equip at least one treat in **/vitrine**, then run **/showoff**. The cooldown is 120 seconds. Filling all three slots gives your team more fighters.

The bot selects a preset enemy team with 60% probability; otherwise it looks for another user’s Vitrine, falling back to a preset if needed. Preset enemies use base treat stats and no plates.

## Combat

Each round, a random living fighter on your team attacks a random living enemy; the enemy then does the same. Your team acts first. Plate passives can heal, reduce damage, revive, or change attacks. A team with no living fighters loses. If both teams survive 13 rounds, the result is a draw.

## Rewards

Winning adds the reward for **each enemy fighter’s rarity**. The total XP reward goes to **every occupied slot**, rather than being split between slots. Milacoins go to your balance.
