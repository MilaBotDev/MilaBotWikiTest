## Serving the daily customer

Open **/customer**, read the clue, and select a discovered treat. There is one attempt per day, using the bot’s fixed UTC+01:00 date. A correct answer gives **5 Expansion Orbs**; a wrong answer removes **5**, stopping at zero, and still uses your attempt. The submitted treat is checked for ownership but is not consumed.

Spend Orbs through the Patisserie upgrade control to bake more treats at once.
