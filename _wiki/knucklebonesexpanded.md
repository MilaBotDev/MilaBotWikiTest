## Start a match

Run **/knucklebonesexpanded**. Choose an optional opponent, a **3×3 or 4×4** board, and whether to enable items. The matching-column scoring and destruction rules from [Knucklebones]({{ '/wiki/knucklebones/' | relative_url }}) still apply.

## Special dice

Each roll has a configured **20%** chance to become a special die.

| Die | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Shielded | Resists destruction once, then becomes an ordinary die of the same value. |
| Sacrificial | Destroys itself, its lane, and one random die on the opponent’s board. |
| Killer | Destroys opposing dice with the same value across the board, then becomes ordinary. |
| Poison | When destroyed, also destroys the die that killed it. |

## Items

With items enabled, the configured item chance is **20%**.

| Item | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Roll Twice Item | Take another turn after placing your die. |
| Bottle of Poison | Choose a lane after placement; a random ordinary die becomes Poison. |
| Shielder Item | Choose a lane after placement; a random ordinary die becomes Shielded. |

Each player can use **Trash once per game** to discard a rolled die. The game ends when either board fills; the higher total score wins.
