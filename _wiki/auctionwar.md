## Playing

Use **/auctionwar** with up to three human opponents; empty seats are filled by bots. Players start with 20 match coins and bid across 10 rounds. These are the auction’s own resources. Read the active auction instructions before bidding. Events change coin counts after each auction.

The most relic points wins; remaining coins break ties.
