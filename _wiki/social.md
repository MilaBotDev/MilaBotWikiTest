## Actions with another user

**/headpat**, **/hug**, **/poke**, **/yuri**, **/lick**, and **/spank** take a target user and send a themed interaction. Their optional **backable** setting controls whether the selected user gets a return-action button. **/hug** also offers a **cuddle** option.

The return button is intended for the selected participant. Use the command directory to check each action’s exact inputs.

## Fortunes and questions

- **/fortunecookie** opens a fortune; its **discreet** option shows the result only to you.
- **/8ball** takes a question and responds with a magic-eight-ball answer.
- **/gaycounter** generates an entertainment result about a selected user. It is not a factual assessment of anyone’s identity.
- **/lgbtqsearch** looks up a term such as `bi` or `hrt` in the bot’s external dictionary. Its **discreet** option limits the response to you.

## Summon a friend

**/summon** takes a target, an optional reason of up to 180 characters, and a style: **Mystic, Dramatic, Cute, Chaotic, or Formal**. It creates a playful ritual message.

## Group prompts and sheep

**/truthordare** opens a prompt board. Its configured default permission is Administrator, and it runs in a server. **/sheep** takes two users and creates a playful shipping image with their avatars and a percentage result.
