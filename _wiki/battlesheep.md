## Start and place

Use **/battlesheep** with an optional opponent; leave it empty to play MilaBot. Each side has a private **6×6 board**, labeled A–F and 1–6.

Use **Open My Board** to access your controls. Choose a position, rotate the flock if needed, and use **Place Flock**. The placement preview distinguishes valid and invalid spaces. **Auto-place Remaining** fills the rest for you.

Each side places five flocks of lengths **4, 3, 2, 2, and 1**, totaling **12 sheep tiles**.

## Attack

Choose an enemy coordinate and press **Send Wolves**. Hits and misses are shown with their own icons. Continue locating the opposing flock while keeping your own positions hidden.

Find all 12 opposing sheep tiles to win.

Use **Refresh** if your private board needs to reflect the latest move. Your opponent has their own private controls; the public game message does not expose their placement board.
