## 3. Keep interactions kind and welcome

You must not use MilaBot to:

- Harass, threaten, discriminate against, impersonate, or deliberately distress someone, including through repeated mentions, summons, social actions, or game invitations.
- Share private information without authority, expose restricted messages, make deceptive quotes, or create humiliating or abusive media about another person.
- Distribute unlawful content, malware, scams, sexual content involving minors, or non-consensual intimate content.
- Bypass permissions, rate limits, access restrictions, cooldowns, or input safeguards; exploit duplicate payouts or purchases; or disrupt the service with automated spam.
- Manipulate gameplay or rankings through exploits, coordinated abuse, or unauthorized automation.
- Use the Bot or its Discord data in ways prohibited by applicable law or Discord’s rules.

Respect a person’s request to stop. Social actions and party prompts are invitations, never permission to cross someone’s boundaries. Nobody has to answer a Truth or Dare prompt or perform a dare. Skip anything unsafe, sexual where prohibited, invasive, or unwelcome, and follow Discord’s age and content restrictions.
