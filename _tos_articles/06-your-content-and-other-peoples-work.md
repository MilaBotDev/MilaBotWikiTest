## 6. Your content and other people's work

You retain any rights you hold in text, messages, images, and other content submitted to the Bot. You give us the limited permission needed to receive, process, transform, and transmit that content to provide the requested feature.

Only submit, copy, quote, or modify material you have the right to use. Access to an avatar, sticker, emoji, or message does not automatically grant permission to republish it. Respect the original creator’s rights and the privacy of the people involved. A wanted poster or quote card must not be presented as an authentic accusation or used to mislead others.

Generated results, messages, and copies may remain in Discord after the original content is changed or deleted. You are responsible for where you request or share them. We do not guarantee that existing messages or content created or sent through the Bot can be removed, particularly where they are no longer within our control or access. Responsibility for managing and removing such content rests with the administrators of the relevant server and/or the person who requested, created, or shared it through the Bot, as applicable.

You may ask us or the relevant server staff for help with problematic Bot content, providing a message link where possible. We may assist with removal where reasonably possible, but cannot guarantee that any particular message, copy, or other content can or will be removed.
