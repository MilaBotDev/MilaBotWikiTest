## 1. Who may use MilaBot

You must be at least 13 and meet any higher minimum age required in your country. If you are below the age at which you can agree to these Terms yourself, your parent or legal guardian must agree on your behalf. Use must also comply with [Discord’s Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).

You must have authority to install the Bot or request administrative actions in a server. Features depend on installation type, server settings, permissions, and service availability.
