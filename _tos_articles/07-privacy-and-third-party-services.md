## 7. Privacy and third-party services

Our [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) describes saved account progress, public rankings, message processing, service providers, and data requests. Some commands publish information about other users or display your gameplay outside your server. Review that policy before using these features.

MilaBot relies on Discord, Cloudflare, and external dictionary or media hosting. Their applicable terms and policies govern their services. External content can be unavailable, inaccurate, or changed by its owner. MilaBot is an independent application and is not endorsed by Discord.
