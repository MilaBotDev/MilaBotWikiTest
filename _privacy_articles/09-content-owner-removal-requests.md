## 9. Content-owner removal requests

If we use your content in MilaBot, including images or GIFs in commands such as "/hug", **we will remove that content from the Bot's configured content and stop using it when you, as its owner or authorized representative, request removal**. Contact the [MilaBot Discord support server](https://discord.gg/Gff3rasUn3) and ask for a private reporting route. Include a link identifying the content and enough information for us to verify ownership or authority. We will also remove copies we host or control. Existing copies in Discord messages or on third-party services may require a separate request to Discord, the relevant server staff, or the hosting provider.
