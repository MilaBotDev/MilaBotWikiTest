## 6. Reasons for processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a lawful basis, we rely on performance of our agreement with you for requested commands and saved gameplay; legitimate interests for operating a secure service, troubleshooting, and processing relevant participant or server information; and legal obligations where disclosure or retention is required by law. Our legitimate interests are service reliability, fair gameplay, and prevention of misuse, subject to your rights and interests.

Using the Bot is voluntary, but a feature may not work without the information it needs. Reading this policy or staying in a server is not blanket consent to unrelated processing. If a future feature requires consent, it must obtain that consent separately.

Automated rules determine game outcomes, reward eligibility, cooldowns, and permission or input checks. The Bot is not intended to make decisions about employment, credit, or other matters with legal or similarly significant effects. Server moderation actions are requested by users with the relevant authority.
