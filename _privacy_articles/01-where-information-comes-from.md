## 1. Where information comes from

We receive information from Discord when you use commands, buttons, or menus; from the options, text, links, and media you submit; and from Discord API requests needed to complete a feature. Another person may select your account for a game, social action, avatar tool, or moderation action, or select your message to quote it.

If you contact us through the support server, we also receive your Discord account details, messages, and any attachments or other information you choose to include. Messages posted in public support channels are visible to people with access to those channels.
