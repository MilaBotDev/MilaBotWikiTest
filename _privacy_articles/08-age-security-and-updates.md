## 8. Age, security, and updates

MilaBot is intended for people who meet Discord’s minimum age requirements, including any higher minimum applicable in their country. Contact us if you believe we hold information about someone below that age so we can investigate and address it.

The Worker verifies Discord interaction signatures, and features use permission and participant checks where appropriate. No service can promise perfect security. Report suspected unauthorized access privately using the instructions in our [Security Policy]({{ '/security/' | relative_url }}), and avoid posting personal information or credentials in public issue reports.

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice, except where notice or consent is required by applicable law. We will revise the "Last updated" date when changes are made. Please review this policy periodically. Updates apply from their stated effective date, subject to any legally required notice or consent. Before using existing information for a new purpose, we will provide any notice and obtain any consent required by applicable law. Updating this policy does not itself provide consent or remove your existing privacy rights.
